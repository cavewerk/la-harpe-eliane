# Angles

The same instrument, framed different ways. Each submission picks one or two
angles, then the claims those angles draw on. Angles are framings, not new
facts — everything they say must already be in core.

### A1 — Computational composite *(backbone)*
La harpe Éliane is a computational composite: computation made perceptible
by expressing it through a transducer into a resonant string. The sound is
the expressional appearance of computation, not a reproduction of a signal.
*Draws on:* T1 · C2 · [instrument.md](instrument.md)
*Suits:* everything — the default spine.

### A2 — Material agency / resistant material
The string is a resistant material that pushes back; agency emerges between
performer and string, and leading passes back and forth. Playing asks for
attention and restraint.
*Draws on:* T2 · T3 · C3 · C4 · C5
*Suits:* residencies, thesis, art-theory and research-creation venues.

### A3 — Resistant-materials taxonomy
The string is one resistant material among many. Materials can be placed on
axes — hysteretic vs path-independent, continuous vs discrete, stable /
unstable / metastable — opening a design space (membranes, rods, plates, air
columns, granular materials).
*Draws on:* T3 · April 2026 outline §5.4.2
*Suits:* grants for future work, design-research venues.

### A4 — Observed behaviours
Documented phenomena: discrete mode jumps instead of glissandi, several upper
modes from one string without the fundamental, beating, hysteresis, seizing
up — and their relation to the open-loop circuit topology.
*Draws on:* T7 · C3 · April 2026 outline §5.3
*Suits:* NIME, ICMC, SMC, technical papers. **Not** for residencies.

### A5 — Radigue lineage
An instrument designed and composed for in the light of Éliane Radigue: her
sound, her ARP 2500 and bowed-harp work, and a deliberate departure from her
feedback practice.
*Draws on:* C1 · C2 · [identity.md](identity.md)
*Suits:* programme notes, festivals, artist statements.

### A6 — Research-creation
Creation as research: knowledge that only emerges through building and
playing the instrument, documented through reflective journaling and
audio-visual records.
*Draws on:* [method.md](method.md)
*Suits:* Hexagram and other research-creation residencies; SSHRC/FRQSC-type
funding. Keep it light — enough to show fluency, not a methods chapter.

### A7 — Translation / transduction
After Benjamin's "The Translator's Task": transduction through the string is
translation, not copying — it gives up literal fidelity to express something
the signal alone does not.
*Draws on:* T4
*Suits:* essays, art writing, catalogue texts.
