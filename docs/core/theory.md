# Theory

Theoretical framing. Every position is marked **settled**, **tentative** or
**parked**, with references. Sources live in
[../thesis/sources/](../thesis/sources/) — see the [index](../thesis/sources/INDEX.md).

## Positions

| # | Position | Status | References |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Computational composites are the backbone.** La harpe Éliane is a composite of computational material (Max additive synthesis), a transducer (driver + electromagnet) and an expressional material (string); sound is the expressional appearance of computation. | settled | Vallgårda 2009; Vallgårda & Redström 2007 |
| T2 | **Material agency, in Malafouris's sense.** Agency is not a property of the performer or the string but emerges *between* them in engagement (cf. the potter's wheel). The string has a lot to offer; who is leading is continually in play. | settled (framing) · tentative (wording) | Malafouris 2008 in Knappett & Malafouris (eds.) |
| T3 | **Resistant material is the concrete, observed form of material agency.** The string selectively reinforces and suppresses, jumps between modes, and remembers prior excitation (hysteresis) — it pushes back. | settled | Thesis outline 2026-04 §5.3–5.4.2 |
| T4 | **Transduction as translation.** Like Benjamin's translator, the instrument does not aim at faithful (high-fidelity) reproduction of the signal; transduction through the string expresses something the signal alone does not. | tentative | Benjamin, "The Translator's Task" (Rendall trans. 1997) |
| T5 | **Reproduction, reframed.** Benjamin's *Work of Art* essay stays, but no longer through the schizophonia lens. New framing **Open** (see questions). | parked | Benjamin 1935 |
| T6 | **Remutualization.** Position relative to Cook's controller/synthesizer critique. Sound comes from the instrument (Cook's third lack addressed); haptic feedback absent (possible low-priority future goal); unpredictability embraced where Cook treats it as distortion. Full position **parked** until the author re-reads Cook. | parked | Cook 2004 |
| T7 | **Open-loop (feed-forward) circuit topology** distinguishes la harpe Éliane from feedback-based actuation (McPherson's magnetic resonator piano; Radigue's feedback practice). A topological distinction at circuit level — distinct from Cook's interaction-level use of "feed-forward". Terminology **parked**. | parked | McPherson 2010; Cook 2004 |
