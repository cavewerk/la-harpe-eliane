# Claims

Numbered, citable claims. Submissions pick from this list; drafts cite claims

Status: **stated** (author's position) · **supported** (backed by reference

### C1
**Lineage.** Éliane Radigue is the primary influence on la harpe Éliane — on
both composition and the design process. The instrument is an ongoing design
project rather than a finished object.
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* Open · *Since:* 2026-10-01

### C2
**Feed-forward.** Where many resonating-string instruments — and Radigue's
own early practice (microphone and oscillator feedback) — rely on feedback,
la harpe Éliane uses a feed-forward chain: oscillator → electromagnet →
string, with no return path.
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* patch architecture ([instrument.md](instrument.md)); Radigue feedback references Open · *Since:* 2026-10-01

### C3
**Instability without feedback.** Despite having no feedback loop, the
instrument's response is unpredictable: a string may resonate and then seize
up, possibly due to hysteresis or residual motion from prior excitation.
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* Open (recordings/measurements; physical mechanism unverified) · *Since:* 2026-10-01

### C4
**Fragility as stance.** This unpredictability is embraced — not as
instability for its own sake, but as a fragility that demands constant
attention and, at once, restraint from the performer.
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* Open · *Since:* 2026-10-01

### C5
**Shared leading.** Playing la harpe Éliane is an entanglement in which
leading passes back and forth — negotiated and shifting — between performer
and string. It is not a contest: the string has much to offer, and restraint
([C4](#c4)) is how the performer makes room for it. Grounded in Malafouris's
material agency ([theory.md](theory.md) T2).
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* Open · *Since:* 2026-10-01

### C6
**New timbres.** The strings determine the instrument's range of expression,
but bringing computational material into the string exposes timbres that
traditional means of excitation (plucking, bowing, striking) cannot reach —
e.g. combinations of upper modes sounding without a strong fundamental.
*Status:* stated · *Evidence:* April 2026 outline §5.3 (upper modes without fundamental); recordings Open · *Since:* 2026-10-04 (Hexagram summary)
